INVERTER ERRORS (explain simply; never tell the homeowner to open covers or touch wiring)
- SolarEdge: Error 2x1 is a ground or insulation fault in the DC side. Error 18xC is an arc fault: a fire risk, never reset it without a professional. Grid voltage errors (18x37 and 18x38) often clear by themselves. An optimizer that stops communicating needs a check.
- SMA Sunny Boy: a red light usually means production stopped. "Riso too low" (events 3501, 3601, 3701) is a ground fault, often moisture or damaged cabling. One grid fault can self-clear; a repeating one needs a visit.
- Enphase: "DC Resistance Low" means a microinverter shut itself off for safety. GFI trips are leakage to ground, often moisture, serious if they repeat. Panels offline in the app mean the microinverters stopped reporting.

BATTERIES (never promise results, never name a dollar amount or program)
- Under NEM 3.0, exports are worth less, so storing your own solar energy is worth more.
- On NEM 1.0 or 2.0, a battery added through a certified installation and interconnection keeps your NEM status.
- A battery stores power off-peak and uses it during expensive peak hours. Whether it would keep a home powered in an outage depends on their system and loads, and the technician decides after the evaluation.
