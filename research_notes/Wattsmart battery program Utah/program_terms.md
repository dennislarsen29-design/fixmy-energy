## Upfront incentive: current amounts and what changed June 15, 2026

### Takeaway
RMP's own page (as summarized by search) says Utah changed effective June 15, 2026, to a flat per-household upfront tier: $2,000 for a battery of 10 kW or larger, $1,000 under 10 kW. The older formula was $400/kW capped at $2,000 per household. Exact post-change wording (and whether it keys on enrollment vs install date) is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- RMP's page says changes to the Utah Wattsmart Battery program take effect June 15, 2026, adjusting residential and commercial incentives — [RMP Wattsmart Battery program](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/wattsmart-battery-program.html) (via search summary)
- RMP page: systems 10 kW or larger capped at $2,000 per household; systems under 10 kW capped at $1,000 — [RMP page](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/wattsmart-battery-program.html) (via search summary)
- Pre-change: RMP/PacifiCorp "Program Administration – Utah Wattsmart Batteries" PDF dated 5/21/2026 lists upfront incentive at $400 per kW of battery output, capped at $2,000 per household — [PacifiCorp PDF](https://www.pacificorp.com/content/dam/pcorp/documents/en/pacificorp/environment/dsm/utah/UT_Program_Administration_Wattsmart_Batteries_25.pdf) (via search summary)
- A tracker describes the post-change schedule as flat amounts decided by the size of one battery, not by how many are installed — [batteryeconomics tracker](https://batteryeconomics.com/state-programs/program-status-tracker.html) (secondary, via search summary)
- FranklinWH: residential applications capped at $2,000 per household since May 1, 2025 — [FranklinWH](https://www.franklinwh.com/homeowner-incentives/wattsmart-battery-program) (secondary)
- Tesla's RMP page: Powerwall 3 owners who submitted their net metering application on or after Sept 1, 2025 can get up to $2,000 upfront ($400/kW, capped per household) — [Tesla RMP page](https://www.tesla.com/support/energy/virtual-power-plant/RockyMountainPower/wattsmart)
- Conflict: one installer blog says a flat $2,000 per system applied as of May 2026 and that the new structure "may include" $1,000 for some under-10 kW systems; the same sources say verify at install time — [byopelectric](https://byopelectric.com/blog/wattsmart-tesla-powerwall-rebate-2026) (secondary)
- Wyoming (not Utah) has its own $1,000/$2,000 tiers dated July 1, 2026 — [Tigo](https://www.tigoenergy.com/bip/rocky-mountain-power-wattsmart-battery-program-wyoming); the $1,000/$2,000 tier may be mistaken for Utah's in some aggregator pages.
- Older figure $150/kW per year of commitment is outdated — [utility program summary via search](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/wattsmart-battery-program.html)
- One tracker says incentive is capped at 70% of equipment cost — [batteryeconomics](https://batteryeconomics.com/state-programs/program-status-tracker.html) (secondary, single source)

### Inferences
- The $2,000 (10 kW+) / $1,000 (<10 kW) tiers appear current for Utah after June 15, 2026. A Powerwall 3 (11.5 kW output) would fall in the 10 kW+ tier, i.e. $2,000 — inferred, not stated by a source.

### Gaps
- Whether the change applies by enrollment/application date or install date: no source found.
- Exact official wording of the new tiers and any grandfathering: official page unreadable.

## Ongoing bill credit

### Takeaway
Reported: $15/kW per year starting in year two, with a cap (reported $100/yr), rising to $50/kW from year five (cap reported $250). Sources conflict on details.

### Cited Findings
- Tracker: residential $15/kW in years 2–4 (up to $100) and $50/kW from year 5 (up to $250) — [batteryeconomics](https://batteryeconomics.com/state-programs/program-status-tracker.html) (secondary)
- Utility document summary: $15/kW annual bill credit during the four-year commitment; $50/kW afterward — [RMP/PacifiCorp via search](https://www.pacificorp.com/content/dam/pcorp/documents/en/pacificorp/environment/dsm/utah/UT_Program_Administration_Wattsmart_Batteries_25.pdf)
- Tesla: starting year two, $15/kW nameplate as monthly bill credit, roughly $172.50 per Powerwall 3 over the four-year term — [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/support/energy/virtual-power-plant/RockyMountainPower/wattsmart)
- Term: 4-year commitment — [KUTV](https://kutv.com/news/eye-on-utah/utah-solar-battery-program-hopes-to-create-virtual-power-plants), [SolarEdge](https://www.solaredge.com/us/incentives/rocky-mountain-power)

### Inferences
- $172.50 over the term = 11.5 kW × $15 × 1 year? (11.5×15 = $172.50 per year) — Tesla's "over the four-year term" wording is ambiguous; per-year reading matches the math.

### Gaps
- Whether the $100/year cap is official: only a secondary tracker; not confirmed on RMP's page.

## Eligibility

### Takeaway
Residential and non-residential RMP customers in Utah; solar not required per one source, but others say the battery must be solar-charged or paired with solar. Sources conflict. New equipment required; approved-battery list at RockyMountainPower.net/battery.

### Cited Findings
- Residential and non-residential RMP customers with Utah service are eligible; no solar required (search summary of Utah program) — [utahcleanenergy.org](https://utahcleanenergy.org/rocky-mountain-power-wattsmart-battery-program/)
- Battery must be on the approved list and be new equipment — [RMP page](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/wattsmart-battery-program.html)
- Conflict: tracker says battery must be charged by on-site solar — [batteryeconomics](https://batteryeconomics.com/state-programs/program-status-tracker.html); SolarReviews says batteries must be paired with solar — [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/blog/rocky-mountain-power-wattsmart-battery-program)
- Powerwall 2 and Powerwall+ not eligible; Powerwall 3 eligible — [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/support/energy/virtual-power-plant/RockyMountainPower/wattsmart)
- Municipal utility homes (e.g., Lehi, Provo) not eligible — [batteryeconomics](https://batteryeconomics.com/state-programs/program-status-tracker.html)

### Gaps
- Rate schedules, min/max size, leased-system rules, retrofit rules: not found.

## Enrollment process

### Takeaway
Per Tigo (Wyoming page, likely similar process): work with an installer, get RMP approval via the customer-generation (interconnection) process BEFORE installing, install, then submit the Wattsmart application. Tesla says the offer appears in the Tesla app within 10 days of the online application.

### Cited Findings
- Steps: installer → RMP approval via customer generation before install → install → Wattsmart application — [Tigo](https://www.tigoenergy.com/bip/rocky-mountain-power-wattsmart-battery-program-wyoming) (Wyoming page; secondary)
- Offer appears in Tesla app within 10 days of online application — [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/support/energy/virtual-power-plant/RockyMountainPower/wattsmart)

### Gaps
- Who must file (customer vs installer), required documents, payment timing/method, installer registration: not found.

## Customer obligations

### Takeaway
4-year commitment; RMP can dispatch the battery; prorated payback if you leave early.

### Cited Findings
- Early exit: prorated payback; 30-day advance notice to withdraw; no payback if the 4-year commitment is completed; must keep internet connection — [SolarEdge](https://www.solaredge.com/us/incentives/rocky-mountain-power) and RMP page via search summary
- Dispatch frequency ~10 events/year per one installer blog — [byopelectric](https://byopelectric.com/blog/add-battery-to-existing-solar-utah) (secondary, unverified)
- Reserve floor conflicts: 20% per one blog; one source says RMP site shows 10% in one place and 20% in another — [byopelectric](https://byopelectric.com/blog/add-battery-to-existing-solar-utah); [search summary](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/wattsmart-battery-program.html)
- Pacific Power (different utility, not Utah) says events may be several per day without notice, ~5 min, under 1 kWh — [Pacific Power details](https://www.pacificpower.net/content/dam/pcorp/documents/en/pacificpower/savings-energy-choices/demand-response/Wattsmart_Battery_Program_Participation_Details.pdf); do not apply to RMP without confirmation.

### Gaps
- No official residential event cap, hours, or opt-out found (the opt-out language found belongs to Wattsmart Business Demand Response). Data-sharing terms not found.
