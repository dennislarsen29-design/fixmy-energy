SDCP Customers Can Get Paid to Add a Battery

Most SDG&E-territory homeowners are automatically on San Diego Community Power (SDCP) rates. If you haven't opted out, you're likely already an SDCP customer — and that makes you eligible for California's Self-Generation Incentive Program (SGIP), a direct upfront rebate based on the size of your battery.

Market Rate $350 / kWh $250 / kWh
Nonmarket Rate (CARE / FERA / Community of Concern) $500 / kWh $350 / kWh

Source: San Diego Community Power — Solar & Battery Savings

CARE / FERA customers
$4,725 back

Do you service systems installed by other companies?
Yes, that is Solar Review's specialty. Any system is serviced, including from companies that have gone out of business, across all major inverter brands.
My solar installer went out of business — can you still help?
Yes — that's our specialty. We regularly service systems from installers that shut down or went bankrupt, including SunPower, Titan Solar, Sunnova, Sullivan Solar Power, Petersen-Dean, Sungevity, Verengo, American Solar Direct, ADT Solar, RGS Energy, Pink Energy, Vision Solar, Lumio, Kota Energy, OneRoof Energy, and Freedom Forever — plus systems financed through Mosaic. Even with the original company gone, your system can still be diagnosed, repaired, monitored, and upgraded, and we work with every major inverter brand — Enphase, SolarEdge, SMA / Sunny Boy, and Tesla.
How do I know if my system is underperforming?
Common signs: bills higher than promised, little change after going solar, or monitoring showing lower production than estimated. A diagnostic evaluation will give you a clear answer.
How does an evaluation help if I'm getting errors on my inverter?
The error can be diagnosed with audits at the panel, AC, and DC levels to pinpoint precisely what's causing it — and what it takes to get the system back up and running.
My SolarEdge inverter is showing an error code or red light — what does it mean?
The most common SolarEdge faults we diagnose are isolation / ground faults (Error 2x1) — an earth-insulation breakdown in the DC wiring, panels, or optimizers; arc-fault detection (Error 18xC, "Arc Detected") — a potential fire risk that should never be reset without a professional; grid voltage out of range (Error 18x37 / 18x38); and power optimizers that stop communicating (the inverter shows a mismatch like "P_OK: 18/20"). An evaluation isolates whether it's at the optimizer, string (DC), inverter, or grid (AC) level.
My SMA Sunny Boy has a red light or a ground-fault / grid-fault event — what's wrong?
A glowing red LED usually means production has stopped. The most common events are insulation / ground faults ("Riso too low," Events 3501, 3601, 3701) — moisture or damaged panels/DC cabling leaking to ground — and grid faults: overvoltage (102), undervoltage (202), voltage-rise protection (301), or frequency disturbance (501). A single grid fault often self-clears; a recurring Riso fault points to real insulation damage. We diagnose whether it's the inverter, the DC array, or a utility condition.
My Enphase panels show offline, or I'm getting "DC Resistance Low" / "GFI" — what does that mean?
With Enphase microinverters, the most common issues are "DC Resistance Low – Power Off" — the microinverter's insulation-resistance sensor dropped below its safe threshold (~7 kΩ) and latched the unit off; GFI ground-fault trips — leakage current to earth, often from moisture but, if it repeats, a genuine insulation fault; and microinverters that stop reporting (panels offline in the Enlighten app). Because every panel has its own microinverter, we can pinpoint exactly which unit(s) failed.
How much does a solar evaluation or diagnostic cost?
The evaluation is free most of the time. A deeper diagnostic can vary depending on your system, warranty status, and the issues involved.
How long does the evaluation take, and do I need to be home?
Evaluations take about 30 minutes on average. So the technician can check the equipment, production, and your consumption, we ask that you're home and present while they're on site.
What happens if you find a problem?
We walk you through exactly what we found and lay out your options — from a simple repair to an inverter swap, warranty replacement, or battery retrofit.
How fast can you come out?
Most evaluations are booked for next-day or within 48 hours.
Do I have to pay anything up front to qualify for a Battery Installation or Retrofit?
A lot of times when qualified, the owner pays little to no money out of pocket. This is based on eligible incentives.
How does battery storage help under NEM 3.0?
NEM 3.0 reduced the value of grid exports. Storing your own solar energy is now far more valuable than selling it back. Upon qualification, battery storage installations can cost $0 using available subsidies — making storage both essential and accessible.
How does a battery help if I'm on NEM 1.0 or NEM 2.0?
Your NEM status stays intact when the battery is added through a certified installation and interconnection process. The battery stores power during off-peak hours and discharges during on-peak — avoiding peak charges, preserving your NEM credits, and even allowing you to send power back to the grid during peak demand (Virtual Power Plant).
What does ongoing monitoring include?
Monthly performance summaries, real-time anomaly alerts, inverter health checks, and cross-referencing of production data against actual utility bills.
Which areas do you serve?
All of Southern California — Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, and San Diego Counties across all major utility territories.
