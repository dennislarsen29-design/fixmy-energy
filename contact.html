What happens next

  1. We call you back. A technician — not a call centre — asks a few questions about your system and your bill.

  2. We book the evaluation. Usually next day. It takes about an hour, it's free, and there's no fee if you need to move it.

  3. You get real numbers. We pull your actual production and set it beside your actual bill. If the system is healthy, we'll tell you that.

Where we work

Solar Review provides solar panel repair, system diagnostics, inverter replacement, monitoring setup and battery installations and retrofits throughout Southern California — including San Diego, Poway, El Cajon, Chula Vista, Carlsbad, Escondido, Santee, Oceanside, La Mesa, Spring Valley and Lakeside, plus Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties.

We service every major brand — SunPower, Sunrun, Enphase, SolarEdge, SMA and Tesla Powerwall — and we specialise in systems whose original installer has closed, including SunPower, Titan Solar, Sunnova, Sullivan Solar Power, Petersen-Dean and Sungevity.
