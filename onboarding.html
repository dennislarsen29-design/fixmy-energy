⚠ California License Requirement

To execute diagnostic and service agreements with customers in California, you must hold an active CSLB Home Improvement Salesperson (HIS) registration.

You can prospect, run appointments, and log leads immediately — but Dennis will deploy all customer agreements until your HIS is confirmed active. The registration is straightforward: apply through the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB), pay the fee, and pass a background check. Total time: 2–4 weeks.
