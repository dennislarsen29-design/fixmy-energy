Common questions

My solar installer went out of business. What happens to my warranty?

Workmanship warranties generally end with the installer, but equipment warranties from the panel, inverter and battery manufacturers usually remain. An independent inspection tells you what is still covered.

Who services my solar system now?

Solar Review services systems from any installer, including the companies listed on this page. We start with your photos and utility bill, then schedule a technician visit or a guided video evaluation.

Is my installer on this list?

If not, it does not matter. We work on any installer's system. This list covers the companies we hear about most.
