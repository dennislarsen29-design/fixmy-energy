Questions

Is Solar Review part of Sunrun?

No. Solar Review is an independent local company. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for Sunrun or Vivint Solar. We work with homeowners who already have solar and want to lower their evening electricity costs.

Do I need Sunrun's permission to add a battery?

No. Our batteries are AC-coupled, meaning they connect at your electric meter and run alongside your existing solar. They don't modify your solar system and don't affect your existing agreement.

Will you change or modify my solar system?

No. AC-coupled batteries connect at the meter and work alongside your existing system. Your panels, inverter and agreement stay as they are. The free evaluation looks at your meter, panel and usage and tells you honestly whether a battery makes sense.

Why do my bills go up in the evening if I have solar?

Solar makes the most power around midday, but San Diego utility rates are highest from 4 to 9 pm, when your panels are producing little. A battery stores midday power so you use it during those expensive hours.

Is the evaluation really free?

Yes. It takes about an hour, there is no obligation, and there is no fee to reschedule. A homeowner should be present.

Are there incentives?

Some homeowners may qualify for utility or program incentives depending on their usage, utility and income. We confirm what may apply during the evaluation, in writing. Nothing is guaranteed in advance.
