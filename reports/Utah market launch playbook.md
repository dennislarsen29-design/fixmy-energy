## Why Black Box is empty, and the fix

Your pipeline is not broken, it is starved. Phase 1c rotates through **46 Utah cities** across your top 8 installers with the Utah state flag, and the saved per-city tally shows nearly every city returning "ok" once and "empty" 7 to 17 times, against many "ok" results for California (internal). The most likely conclusion is that PermitStack returns essentially no Utah permits; whether it covers Utah at all is unverified, and web search found nothing about the product. So stop expecting the nightly run to fill Utah and test it deliberately with one live call.

Permit data is likely the slow path anyway. Salt Lake City publishes active permits on Utah's open data portal ([opendata.utah.gov](https://opendata.utah.gov/salt-lake-city)) and via SLCPermits.com ([slc.gov](https://www.slc.gov/buildingservices/how-to-research-building-permits-online/)), but no solar-specific bulk dataset for any Utah jurisdiction turned up. Shovels advertises 1,800+ jurisdictions via API, but its prices are quote-gated and its Utah solar coverage is unconfirmed ([Shovels docs](https://shovels.redoc.ly)). For owners, California's county-roll workaround has no Utah twin yet; a commercial Salt Lake County parcel file is listed at **$250 with about 98% owner coverage and 393,521 parcels** ([ReportAll](https://reportallusa.com/store/Utah/49035)). Two catches: a parcel file has no solar flag, so it must be joined to permits or installer lists, and its licensing terms were not verified.

The best Utah-specific targeting is the orphan list. Documented Utah-linked failures include Lumio (Ch. 11, 2024) ([Solar Builder](https://solarbuildermag.com/news/residential-solar-player-lumio-files-for-bankruptcy/)), Sunworks and Solcius (Provo, Ch. 7) ([Solar Power World](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2025/04/qa-new-solar-service-provider-takes-on-titan-sunworks-customers/)), PureLight Power (shut late 2025) ([Solar Power World](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2025/12/26/residential-installer-purelight-power-shutters-operations-blames-obbba/)), Legend Solar (2018) ([SL Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/2018/03/21/customers-across-three-states-may-be-out-thousands-of-dollars-as-utah-based-legend-solar-shows-signs-of-crumbling/)), and G3 Solar of Salt Lake City (Ch. 7, June 2026) ([docket](https://www.inforuptcy.com/browse-filings/utah-bankruptcy-court/2:26-bk-23517/bankruptcy-case-g3-solar-llc)). Customer counts are not published. Skip tracing is cheap on paper (Tracerfy advertises about $0.02 per hit, [Tracerfy](https://tracerfy.com/)), but your own experience contradicts the headline: the trace you used cost 2 credits per lead and returned no Do Not Call data. So buy a separate scrub before any dial. A realistic one-week target of 1,000+ addresses depends entirely on a permit vendor covering Utah; without one, expect a few hundred parcel-plus-installer matches.

## Doors and Dialing need a lead pool and a license answer

Doors builds a GPS route from Black Box leads with coordinates, and Dialing queues leads with phones; both depend on the pool above (internal). Six houses is not a route. The rules are also local and unresolved. Utah gives home-solicitation buyers until the end of the third business day to cancel ([Utah Code 70C-5](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title70C/Chapter5/C70C-5-S102_1800010118000101.pdf)), and failing to give that notice on a direct solicitation sale is a deceptive practice ([R152-11-9](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/utah/Utah-Admin-Code-R152-11-9)). Your Utah contract and any Utah-bound agreement should carry the statutory notice language ([13-11-4](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter11/C13-11-S4_2026050620260506.pdf)). Salt Lake City requires a solicitor either to hold a business license or work for a licensed business, and applicants upload a criminal background check report ([SLC Finance](https://www.slc.gov/Finance/?p=416)). Rules for Sandy, Draper, West Jordan, South Jordan, Lehi, Provo and Orem were not found, and no source gave knocking hours or the effect of a "No Soliciting" sign. So the practical stance: get the SLC answer, call each suburb's licensing office before first knock, and lead with proof of license and registration, because Utah homeowners have recent memory of failed installers and aggressive door sellers ([SL Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/business/2024/03/19/utah-customers-are-still-paying)).

Dialing has its own Utah rules. Calls to residential or cell numbers without prior express consent are barred from 9 p.m. to 8 a.m., on Sundays and on holidays ([Utah Code 13-25a-103](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter25A/C13-25a-S103_2022050420220504.html)). Most telephone sellers must register annually with a bond under the Telephone Fraud Prevention Act ([Division page](https://dcp.utah.gov/for-businesses/seller-telephone-soliciting/)); a third-party source says SB 38 tightened this from May 6, 2026, allowing only a surety bond or certificate of deposit ([Utah DCP](https://commerce.utah.gov/dcp/for-businesses/seller-telephone-soliciting/apply-for-registration/), snippet only). Exemptions for calling your own prior or permit lists were not determined. Until you know, cold-calling Utah homes from California is the riskiest thing on this plan.

## Outsource dialing only after a clean pilot

Pricing is vendor-blog planning ranges, not quotes. A dedicated Philippine setter runs about **$8.50 to $14.50/hr versus $25 to $45/hr for a US setter**, with a 1 to 2 agent offshore pilot at roughly **$1,200 to $5,000 a month** ([VA Masters](https://vamasters.com/how-much-does-appointment-setter-cost/), [Salesbread](https://salesbread.com/appointment-setting-services-cost/)). Per-appointment deals run $15 to $100 generically ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/outsourced-appointment-setting-service-guide/)); one solar freelancer lists $80 per appointment and $190 per sit ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/kousargroup1/selling-solar-appointments-and-sits)), which is a listing, not a benchmark. Inbound answering services like Smith.ai and Ruby are not outbound dialers. Vendors surfaced but unverified for solar outbound: Hit Rate Solutions (from $7/hr, [Clutch](https://www.clutch.co/profile/hit-rate-solutions?page=1)), Outsource BPO and Flatworld Solutions; the evidence does not support recommending any of them. Choose hourly over per-appointment for the pilot so you learn your own conversion, and track held appointments, not sets. Published show-rate ranges of 65 to 80% are vendor targets and probably overstate cold permit-list calling ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/solar-home-energy-growth/solar-sales-kpis-metrics)).

Treat the seller as liable for the vendor's calls. Contract for: Utah registration or coverage, scrub against the National registry and your internal list before every dial, no auto-dialed or prerecorded calls or texts to cells without consent, recordings kept, indemnity and audit rights. The simplest integration is a restricted GoHighLevel or dialer login that books onto your shared evaluation calendar ([GHL roles](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002544-sub-account-user-roles-permissions-and-assigned-data)). The cheaper control group is 1 to 2 local part-time setters on your own dialer, which already has scripts, AI notes and a callback queue (internal).

Confirm on the official page before quoting.

## Compliance items to confirm before selling or calling

- Does a repair/battery/diagnostics company count as a "residential solar panel retailer" under HB 57 (registration required from July 1, 2026, per [Utah Commerce](https://commerce.utah.gov/dcp/residential-solar-panel-retailer/))? Bond tiers are reported as $100k/$200k/$300k in one note but the dollar amounts were not confirmed.
- Does the W-2 sales-rep requirement survive in the enacted text, and does it reach your 1099 reps? A 2026 follow-up bill (HB 428) appears stalled ([Better Utah](https://billtracker.betterutahinstitute.org/bills/HB0428)).
- Does a seller that subcontracts install need its own Utah contractor license? The solar PV classification (S202) and electrical (E200) rules are in [R156-55a-301](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/utah/Utah-Admin-Code-R156-55a-301); the seller question is unresolved.
- Telemarketing registration, bond and exemptions; Do Not Call scrub method.
- SLC solicitor license plus each suburb's rules; three-business-day notice in the Utah contract.
- Recording: Utah is reported one-party consent ([Utah Code 77-23a](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title77/Chapter23A/C77-23a_1800010118000101.pdf)), but California all-party rules apply to calls with California residents.
- Have a Utah attorney review the vendor contract and confirm the points above.

## Conclusion

The surprising finding is that the Utah gap is a data problem and a permission problem, not a staffing one: you cannot dial or knock a pool that holds six houses, and you may not yet be allowed to. That reorders the week. Spend it on appointments, on one decisive PermitStack test, and on written answers from the state, then decide whether Black Box, a call center, or purchased leads should be the volume engine. Because Utah compliance is currently parked as a low-priority watch item in your portal, consider raising it for the launch period, since an unregistered sale is reportedly a separate violation each time.
