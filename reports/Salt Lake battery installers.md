## Utah rules to confirm (unverified)
- Rocky Mountain Power: Schedule 137 export credit resets each March 1; 2026 update (docket 26-035-T03) lowered it, cents/kWh not retrieved. Wattsmart Battery best-supported figure $1,000 (<10 kW) / $2,000 (≥10 kW) from July 1, 2026, from one installer page; conflicting older figures; no official RMP page found.
- Federal 30% residential credit (25D) reportedly ended 12/31/2025, batteries included; third-party-owned may still use 48E. No Utah state battery credit found (not confirmed absent).
- HB 57 (2025): solar retailer registration with the Division of Consumer Protection from July 1, 2026, ~$100k+ surety. Whether sell-and-subcontract is covered, and any W-2-only sales-rep rule, are unconfirmed.
- DOPL: sources conflict whether S202 (Solar PV) covers batteries or an E200/E201 electrical license is needed.
- Cancel right: 3 business days (Utah Code 70C-5-102/103, R152-11-9). City solicitor permits exist (Lehi etc.); nothing found for South Jordan/SLC.
