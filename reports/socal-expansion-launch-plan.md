# Southern California launch plan: Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Meta (2026-10-27)

Scope: Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino and Imperial counties, on top of San Diego. Electricians are covered in Calexico, Orange County and the Inland Empire. Everything below is a plan for Dennis to approve. Budgets are starting points to test, not forecasts.

## 1. Google Business Profile (one profile, widened service area)
- Keep ONE profile (Dennis's decision). Add service areas by county or main city: Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, Imperial (Calexico, El Centro). Do not add a fake street address.
- Description addition: "We also serve Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino and Imperial counties. We start with your photos and utility bill, then arrange a technician visit or a guided video evaluation."
- Website link goes to the hub page `/southern-california-solar-repair`. Add a GBP post per county linking to its page (`/solar-repair-<county>-county`).
- Request indexing for the new pages in Search Console, about 5 a day: `/southern-california-solar-repair`, the four county pages, `/solar-repair-imperial-county`.

## 2. Google Ads
- One campaign per county, same ad groups: "solar repair", "solar inverter not working", "[defunct installer] service" (SunPower, Titan, Sunnova, Simply Solar, Solcius, Freedom Forever).
- Location targeting: "people in" the county only (not "interested in").
- Landing page: the matching county page, not the homepage.
- Conversion: confirm the `/booked` Schedule conversion is the optimized action. Start at $20-30 a day per county; judge after 14 days on cost per booked evaluation.
- Imperial County is small. Fold it into one "Imperial + Coachella Valley" test at the lowest budget.
- Call extension and call tracking on. Phone clicks already fire GA4 and Meta Contact.

## 3. Meta
- Use the installer-name hook ("Is your solar installer still in business?"), vertical video first, static second.
- One audience per county, Advantage+ broad, 18+ homeowners. Exclude existing customers (customer list upload).
- Optimize for Schedule; $20-40 a day per county for at least 7 days before judging.
- If a campaign is flagged as a Special Ad Category, appeal it; do not assume it is permanent.

## 4. Electrician coverage to confirm before spending
Spend only where a partner can take the Diagnostic. Still needed from Dennis: partner name, counties covered, flat fee agreed, response time promise. Until then, advertise evaluation-first (free evaluation, virtual or in person).

## 5. How we will know (built today)
Admin, SEO Pulse now shows "Leads, bookings & revenue by county, 90 days" under the by-channel table. Review weekly. A county with leads but no bookings means a page or follow-up problem; bookings but no sold jobs means a Diagnostic coverage problem.
