Service Areas

Solar Review provides solar panel repair, solar system diagnostics, and battery installations and retrofits throughout California, including San Diego, Poway, El Cajon, Chula Vista, Carlsbad, Escondido, Santee, Oceanside, La Mesa, Spring Valley, and Lakeside. We service all solar brands — SunPower, Sunrun, Enphase, SolarEdge, SMA, and Tesla Powerwall — and we're an approved installer for San Diego Community Power (SDCP) battery storage rebates under the Self-Generation Incentive Program (SGIP). Ask about the SDCP battery rebate when you book your free evaluation.
