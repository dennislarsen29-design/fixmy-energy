FixMy.Energy Diagnostic Service Agreement

This agreement is between the homeowner identified above ("Customer") and FixMy.Energy ("Company").

1. Services. Company will perform a residential electrical and solar/battery system diagnostic evaluation at the Customer's property. The evaluation includes an inspection of the main service panel, sub-panels, existing solar or battery equipment, and a written assessment of findings.

2. Diagnostic Fee. Customer agrees to pay the diagnostic fee shown above. This fee is due at the time of scheduling and is non-refundable except as provided in Section 3.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellation. Customer may reschedule at no charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Rescheduling with less than 24 hours' notice will incur a $50 rescheduling fee. No-shows without contact forfeit the full diagnostic fee.

4. No Obligation. The diagnostic fee does not obligate the Customer to purchase any additional products or services. Any follow-on work will be presented in a separate proposal that Customer may accept or decline.

5. Authorization. Customer authorizes Company's technician to access the property and electrical equipment solely for the purpose of the diagnostic evaluation.

6. Limitation of Liability. Company's liability is limited to the amount of the diagnostic fee paid.

⚠️ Rescheduling within 24 hours incurs a $50 fee. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance to reschedule at no charge.
